📱 Wapas App Par Jayein
1

Mobile Mein App Install Karna (Bina PlayStore Ke)

Yeh app aik modern Progressive Web App (PWA) hai, jo mobile ki memory ko zaya kiye baghair seedha install ho jati hai:

  • Android Mobile: Chrome browser mein link open karein. Upar 3 dots (⋮) par click karein aur "Install app" ya "Add to Home screen" select karein.
  • iPhone (Apple): Safari browser mein open karein. Neeche Share button (⎋) daba kar "Add to Home Screen" par tap karein.
💡 Fayda: App aap ke mobile par icon ban kar aa jayegi aur agay se bina browser search kiye 1-click par open hogi.
2

Pehli Dafa Account & Family Group Bananna (Head / Sarbarah)

Agar aap pehli dafa ghar ka khata shuru kar rahe hain:

  • App open kar ke "Naya Account Banayein" par click karein.
  • Apna Mobile Number (maslan: 03091195695), apna Naam, aur Password darj karein.
  • Family Ka Naam: Apne ghar ya khandaan ka naam likhein (maslan: Malik Family ya Khan House).
  • Apna Rishta chunein (maslan: Walid / Head).
  • "Profile Banayein" par click karte hi aap ka account ban jayega aur aap ko aik 6-Digit Family Invite Code (maslan: 915964) mil jayega.
🔑 Ahem Point: Yeh 6-digit Invite Code apne ghar walon (bhai, walid, bachon) ko share karein taake wo aap ki family mein shamil ho sakein.
3

Ghar Walon Ka Shamil Hona (Family Tree Directory)

Ghar ke doosre afraad ko shamil karne ka intehai aasan tareeqa:

  • Doosra fard app open kar ke "Naya Account Banayein" par click kare.
  • Apna Mobile Number aur Naam likhein.
  • Family Join Code: Yahan wahi 6-digit code likhein jo Head ko mila tha.
  • Apna rishta chunein (maslan: Bara Bhai, Chota Bhai, Beta, Walida).
🌳 Ab Family Tree tab mein sab members ke card, naam, phone number aur role aik sath nazar aayeinge.
4

🔒 Zaati Private Khata (Other Khata — 100% Khufia)

Yeh hisaab aap ka zaati wallet hai jo ghar ke kisi doosre member ko kabhi nazar nahi aayega:

  • 3 Grand Total Cards: Upar baray cards mein saaf likha hoga:
    • Kul lene hain (Sab logon se)
    • Kul dene hain (Sab logon ko)
    • Net Hisaab (Overall balance).
  • Person Wise Balance: Har shakhs (maslan: Ali, Usman, Saman Wala) ka alag card banega jis par unka net balance aur status likha hoga.
  • Entry karne ke liye "+ Zaati Entry Likhein" par click karein.
🛡️ Pori Privacy: Yeh record strictly aap ki apni ID se lock hota hai, is liye ghar ka koi doosra member isay nahi dekh sakta.
5

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Shared Khata (Ghar Ka Lena Dena)

Agar koi aisi entry hai jo poore ghar ke ilm mein honi chahiye (maslan kisi rishtedar ya dukaandar se ghar ke liye raqam li ya di):

  • Family Khata tab mein ja kar "+ Family Entry Likhein" par click karein.
  • Audit Trail Badge: Har entry ke neeche darj karne wale ka naam likha hoga:
    👤 Entry darj ki: Muhammad Asad (Chota Bhai)
  • Is se ghar mein kisi kism ki ghalat fehmi ya hisaab ka jhagda nahi hota.
6

💰 Ghar Ka Budget & Animated Maahana Muqabla

Ghar ki aamdan aur kharchey ka hisaab rakhne ka tareeqa:

  • Ghar Ka Budget tab mein ja kar Aamdan (Income) aur Kharch (Expense) darj karein (maslan: Tankhwah, Rashan, Bijli Bill, School Fee).
  • Animated Dashboard: Main screen par animated bars aamdan aur kharche ka muqabla karke dikhayengi ke kitni bachat bachi.
  • Month Rollover: Mahina khatam hone par app pochay gi ke unspent bachat aglay mahine ke balance mein shamil karni hai ya nahi.
7

🤝 Kameti / Committee Tracker

Agar aap ne ya ghar walon ne koi committee dali hui hai:

  • Committee / Kameti tab mein "+ Nayi Kameti Shuru Karein" dabayein.
  • Kul Raqam (Total Pool), Maahana Qist, Kul Mahinay, aur "Meri Baari Ka Mahina" darj karein.
  • App har mahine track karegi ke kitni qistain ada ho chuki hain aur committee kab milni hai.
8

✏️ Ghalat Entry Ko Theek Karna (Edit & Delete)

Agar koi raqam ya naam ghalti se enter ho jaye:

  • Har entry card ke right side par do buttons hain:
    • `✏️ Edit`: Click karne par wahi form khul jata hai. Raqam ya note theek kar ke "Update Karein" dabayein.
    • `🗑️ Delete`: Click karne par entry mukammal tor par delete ho jati hai aur grand totals update ho jatay hain.
9

📷 Profile Photo Lagana aur 🔐 Password Badlna

Apni profile ko personalize aur secure karein:

  • Settings tab mein ja kar `📷 Tasveer Lagayein` dabayein aur camera ya gallery se photo select karein. Yeh photo header aur Family Tree mein nazar aayegi.
  • Password Badlna: Settings mein "Password Tabdeel Karein" form mein purana password daal kar naya password set kar sakte hain.
10

📥 Mobile Mein Offline Backups (Excel & PDF)

Khata kabhi zaya na ho, is ke liye Settings tab mein 1-click downloads mojood hain:

  • 📄 Complete PDF Report: Saaf-suthri A4 size PDF report download karein, jo mobile mein khul sakti hai ya print ho sakti hai.
  • 📊 Excel Workbook (.xls): Excel aur Google Sheets mein khulne wali file download karein.
  • 📥 JSON Data Backup: Complete raw data copy mobile mein mehfooz karein.
✅ Behtareen Mashwara: Har mahine ke aakhir mein PDF ya Excel backup download kar ke WhatsApp ya Google Drive par save kar lein.
